TOXCARD — Privacy Policy

Effective 16 September 2026

TOXCARD is a clinical reference application compiled by Jae Hyek Choi, MSc, PhD, DVSc. This document describes what data the app collects — the short answer is none.

What the app does with your data

Nothing. TOXCARD does not collect, transmit, store on any server, sell, or share any information about you or your device. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no crash reporting.

Data stored locally on your device

The app remembers small conveniences in your browser's or app's own local storage on your device:

  • Your saved (starred) entries
  • Recently viewed entries
  • Your weight and height (if entered for the dose calculator)
  • Your theme preference

This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or anyone else. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app deletes it.

Network

TOXCARD works entirely offline. The only network activity is an occasional check for a newer reviewed dataset when the app is opened online; this fetch retrieves public reference content and sends no information about you.

Third parties

None. The app uses no third-party SDKs, analytics services, advertising networks, or crash-reporting services.

Children

Not directed at children. Intended for use by trained healthcare professionals.
