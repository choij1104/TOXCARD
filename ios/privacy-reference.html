TOXCARD — Privacy Policy

Effective 26 September 2026 · iOS app

TOXCARD is a clinical toxicology reference published by HAKOYA LLC. This policy describes what data the app collects. The short answer is none.

What the app does with your data

Nothing. TOXCARD does not collect, transmit, store on any server, sell, or share any information about you or your device. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no crash reporting.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps a few conveniences in its own storage on your device:

  • Your saved (starred) entries
  • Recently viewed entries
  • Your theme preference

This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Network

None. The reference content, fonts, and every screen are bundled inside the app. TOXCARD makes no network requests and works the same in airplane mode from the first launch.

Third parties

None. The app uses no third-party SDKs, analytics services, advertising networks, or crash-reporting services.

Children

Not directed at children. Intended for use by trained healthcare professionals.
