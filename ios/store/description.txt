TOXCARD is a clinical toxicology reference for emergency physicians, toxicologists, pharmacists, nurses and paramedics. Every entry is one card: what the patient took, what you give, and the pitfall that harms the patient if you get it wrong.

ONE CARD PER POISON
• 187 toxins: drugs, envenomations, mushrooms and plants, household and industrial agents, chemical and fire casualties
• Agent, dose and management, and the pitfall on the same screen. Nothing clinically important is hidden behind a tap
• Evidence tier on every card, from FDA-approved specific antidote to supportive care only, with a badge that never overstates it
• Time-critical window where acting inside a stated time changes the outcome
• Named sources on every card, with review dates printed in the app

EVERY ANTIDOTE, AND WHAT IT TREATS
• 79 agents with brand names and class
• Open an agent to see every toxin it is used for; open a toxin to see every agent involved

TOXIDROME TRIAGE
• Eight patterns for the patient with no history
• Each names the finding that separates it from the one it is confused with: dry versus wet skin for anticholinergic against sympathomimetic, rapid clonus against lead-pipe rigidity for serotonin syndrome against neuroleptic malignant syndrome

BUILT FOR IPHONE
• iPhone Search: every toxin and antidote appears in Spotlight, and a result opens its card
• Siri and Shortcuts: "Look up an antidote in TOXCARD", "Look up a toxin in TOXCARD", "Open toxidrome triage in TOXCARD"
• Home Screen quick actions: Search, Toxidrome Triage and your latest pinned cards
• A–Z section index, scoped search by toxin, antidote, brand or toxidrome, and a category browser
• Share a card as text or print it with AirPrint
• Pin the cards you need; recently viewed cards are kept on the device
• Light and dark appearance, haptic feedback, swipe back

OFFLINE AND PRIVATE
• Every card is inside the app and works in airplane mode from the first launch
• No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. Nothing leaves your iPhone

Doses are shown exactly as their sources state them. Confirm every dose against your own formulary before administration.

TOXCARD is decision support for trained clinicians. It is not a protocol and not a substitute for a regional poison center. In the United States, call Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222.
