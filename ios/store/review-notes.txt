TOXCARD 1.1 is a substantial update made in response to the Guideline 4.3 and 4.2.6 review of 1.0.0 on October 16, 2026.

WHAT CHANGED IN 1.1 (native iOS features written for this app in Swift)
1. iPhone Search (Core Spotlight). All 187 toxins and 79 antidote agents are indexed on the device with their synonyms and brand names. Tapping a Spotlight result opens that card in TOXCARD; "Search in App" from Spotlight opens TOXCARD's search with the same text. The card currently open is also offered to Siri Suggestions (NSUserActivity).
2. Siri and Shortcuts (App Intents, App Shortcuts on iOS 17 and later). "Look up an antidote in TOXCARD", "Look up a toxin in TOXCARD", "Open toxidrome triage in TOXCARD", "Search TOXCARD". The antidote and toxin entities resolve by name, synonym or brand (for example "Cyanokit") and open the card on screen.
3. Home Screen quick actions. Touch and hold the icon: Search, Toxidrome Triage, and up to two of the user's pinned cards (set dynamically).
4. Share sheet and AirPrint for a single card, haptic feedback, window appearance that follows the in-app theme (status bar and keyboard match), left-edge swipe back.
5. New iPhone interface and identity: four-tab bar (Cards, Antidotes, Triage, Pinned), large titles, inset grouped lists, an A–Z section index, a settings sheet, a new tier "card tab" design, system fonts, a new color system, and a new app icon. The clinical content and its sources are unchanged.

HOW TO TEST
- No account, sign-in or network is needed. The app works in airplane mode.
- Launch, tap Continue. Tap the "Cyanide" card under Time-critical: agent, dose, pitfall and sources are on one screen. Tap the star to pin it, the share icon to share it, and the "..." button for Print card.
- Spotlight: after the first launch, return to the Home Screen, swipe down and search "hydroxocobalamin" or "Cyanokit". The TOXCARD result opens the Hydroxocobalamin card.
- Siri: say "Look up an antidote in TOXCARD", then "Naloxone". Or open the Shortcuts app > App Shortcuts > TOXCARD.
- Quick actions: touch and hold the TOXCARD icon. After pinning a card it appears there too.
- Settings (gear icon, top right): appearance, the Search index status and a rebuild button, and the dataset review dates.

REFERENCE BUILD
Doses are displayed exactly as their sources state them; this build performs no dose calculation (Guideline 1.4.2). It is intended for trained clinicians and states that it is not a substitute for a poison center.

ORIGINALITY
TOXCARD is a standalone app. It shares no code with any other app on this account beyond Capacitor and Apple's frameworks. It was not built from a template or with an app-generation service. Its dataset (toxins, antidote agents, protocols, toxidromes) was compiled and reviewed for this app, and its native code (Spotlight indexing, App Intents, quick actions) exists only in TOXCARD.
